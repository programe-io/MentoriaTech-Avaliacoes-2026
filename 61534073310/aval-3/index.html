❓ Frequently Asked

Can a small business really beat a big brand?+
Not by being bigger — by being closer. Small businesses win on relationship, agility, and authenticity. You don't need to beat them at their game; you need to make them unable to play yours.
What if I can't compete on price?+
Then don't. Compete on experience, service, and story. Customers pay more when they feel seen, understood, and cared for. Price is the last thing loyal customers think about.
How do I find my niche?+
Start with what only you can say. Look for the customers who already love you most, and ask what specifically they value. Your niche is usually hiding in that answer.
Is social media worth it for a local business?+
Yes — but only if you show up as yourself. Post the owner's face, the daily reality, the wins and failures. Algorithms reward authenticity, and customers reward it even more.
What's the single biggest mistake small businesses make?+
Trying to be a smaller version of a big brand. When you copy their playbook, you lose the one advantage you actually have: proximity to your customer.
